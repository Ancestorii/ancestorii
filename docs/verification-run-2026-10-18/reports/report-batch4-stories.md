### Fixed

Speed (item 1). Round trips counted as sequential network waits to Supabase from the server render:

- `app/stories/page.tsx` (feed). Before: 17 sequential waits for an anonymous visitor (stories, media, reactions, comments, Profiles, N avatar signs, featured, featured media, featured reactions, featured comments, featured Profiles, N signs, 50 trending candidates, candidate reactions, trending media, trending comments, topic counts), 19 signed in (plus getUser and own Profiles). After: the public lists come from `getPublicFeed()` (`src/lib/stories/publicFeed.ts`), one `unstable_cache` entry shared by every visitor, revalidated every 60 s and dropped at once on publish/delete (tag `stories`). On a miss it is 2 waits: phase 1 runs the four lists side by side, each story carrying its media and its like and comment counts as PostgREST embeds (`reaction_count:story_reactions(count)`), phase 2 reads the authors' `profile_cards` in one query. On a hit: 0. `auth.getUser()` runs side by side with the feed (and costs no network call at all for a visitor without a session cookie); a signed-in reader then pays one more wait for their own name, side by side with avatar signing. Net: anonymous 17 to 0 (2 on a miss), signed in 19 to 2 (3 on a miss).
- `app/stories/[slug]/page.tsx` (story). Before: generateMetadata 2 waits plus the page 15 (story, media, reaction count, comment count, own reaction, Profiles, sign, comments, commenter Profiles, N signs, related by topic, backfill, related media, related reactions/comments). Phase 3: avatar signing (cached, below). Counts and comments are not cached between requests on purpose: the reader who just liked or commented sees it on reload.
