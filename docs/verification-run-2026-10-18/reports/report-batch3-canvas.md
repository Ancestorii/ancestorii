### Left for the owner
- **Empty slots print the placeholder.** In export mode an empty slot still renders the dashed box and the words "Choose an image from the media tab" (`Slot.tsx:118-146`, print pipeline, not changed). The preview shows the same, so WYSIWYG holds, but a half-filled £130 canvas would be printed with that text. Options: (a) block ordering until every photo slot is filled, (b) render empty slots as plain white in export. I recommend (a) plus (b); I only blocked zero-photo orders.
- **Layout switch deletes every placed photo without asking** (`Canvasbuilder.tsx:59`). Recommend a confirmation dialog when photos are placed.
- **Deleting a canvas deletes its orders.** `canvas_orders.canvas_id` is `ON DELETE CASCADE` (verified live), so a paid order disappears from history if the canvas is deleted. Recommend blocking deletion once an order exists (or `SET NULL`).
- **The library's "Rotate 180°" does not reach the print.** The flag is stored and the thumbnail rotates, but neither the stage nor the export applies `rotation` (`Slot.tsx`, consistent on both sides). Either apply it in the renderer (print change) or remove the button from this editor.
- **Checkout currency is guessed from the browser's timezone/language** (`Canvasheader.tsx:47-58`); the currency picker in the create drawer is display-only. A US visitor with an en-GB browser pays GBP. Recommend persisting the drawer's choice or a currency picker at order time.
- **The "Save" button is cosmetic** (`Canvaseditor.tsx:218-223`: waits 500 ms and shows "Saved!"). Everything saves on action. Kept because Books/Acrylic do the same; consider removing or wiring it to the caption flush.
- **Journal progress caps at 50%** (slot 1 is text-only but counts as a slot). Cosmetic.
- **Export pages are public by UUID** (`/export/canvas/[id]` uses the service role and the middleware does not cover `/export`). This is required so Browserless can render without cookies; the UUID is the only protection. Acceptable, but worth knowing.
- **Prodigi SKU orientation**: `GLOBAL-CAN-16X36/20X32/24X72` are used for both orientations with `sizing: fillPrintArea`. Prodigi's public pages do not state whether one SKU covers both; confirm with Prodigi before the first portrait order ships.
- **Express shipping (noted earlier)**: for canvases the webhook stores the chosen method and the fulfil route passes it (`shipping_method || 'Standard'`), so canvas Express is correct; the Books fulfil route still hard-codes `'Standard'` (`app\api\fulfill\[bookId]\route.ts:427`, owner said leave it).
