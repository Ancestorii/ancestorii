### The /features layout shift (0.10)
I traced every element the hub renders and nothing in its own markup changes after load: no client component in the article body except `Fold` (server renders `<details open>`, stays open at 1280, no state flip), no image without a reserved box (PhotoFrame is an aspect-ratio box with a `fill` image), no `Date`/`random`/`window` branches, no details toggles (FAQ is `variant="open"`), no sticky bar (the header is `position: relative`), no scrollbar change. At 1280x900 the hub's first viewport is the hero only (header 88px, crumbs, a 3-line h1, a 430-character lead, a second paragraph, the button: about 850px), so the thing that moved is text.

The only mechanism left is the web-font swap on a cold cache, and the hub is unusually exposed to it: `.heroLeft .heroTitle { max-width: 16ch }` (site.module.css:76) is a font-relative width. During the fallback period `16ch` is 16 zero-widths of size-adjusted Arial; when Plus Jakarta Sans arrives the box itself changes width (roughly 40px at 64px type), the `text-wrap: balance` title re-wraps, and with a long title (49 characters here, 66 on `/compare/ancestorii-vs-ancestry`, the other outlier) a line is gained or lost, pushing the 8-line lead and the button down by one title line. That alone is about 0.05 of CLS at 900px, plus the lead's own reflow. Short-titled feature pages escape it, and any page loaded second has the font cached, which matches "hub 0.10, ancestry 0.12, everything else near 0". `ReasonCards` is the only kit piece shared by exactly those two pages and no feature page; I read it twice and it has nothing that moves (static grid, fixed-size SVG icons, transforms only).

How the lead can confirm in two minutes: (1) hard-reload `/features` twice with the cache on; the second load should be near 0. (2) With DevTools "Disable cache" on, load `/features/voice-notes` first; if it now reports a similar shift, it is the cold font swap, not the hub. (3) In the Performance trace, the layout-shift entry's culprit node will be the hero h1 or lead.

- **Typeface loading choice (zero-shift vs. always-on-brand).** The fix for the hub's shift is in two shared files (below). The stronger option, `display: 'optional'` on Plus Jakarta Sans, removes the swap entirely, but on a visitor's very first cold view the page may stay in the fallback typeface if the font is not ready within about 100ms. The gentler option, replacing the `16ch` cap with an em value, keeps the font swap but stops the title box changing width, which removes most of the shift. I recommend doing the `16ch` change now and trying `optional` only if the lead still measures shift.
- **Question-link lifetime is vaguer than it needs to be.** `family-questions` says links are "time-limited"; the app's `/q/[code]` page and `sent_prompts.expires_at` say 7 days. Not wrong, but "valid for 7 days" would be more useful. Copy decision, so I left it.
- **One title runs slightly long.** "Shared Family Photo Albums with Stories and Voices" becomes 62 characters once the root template adds " | Ancestorii". Fine for Google at this letter width, but if you want every title under 60, trim two or three words.
